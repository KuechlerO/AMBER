Screen enrichment plots (optional)

These plots show log-fold-change (LFC) enrichment from the NGG base-editing screen in Schmidt et al., Nature (2024) — a large-scale mutagenesis map of T cell–function genes in primary human T cells. Panels cover TNFα, IFNG, PD1, and CD25 readouts (ABE and CBE). Guide sequences and mapped edit sites come from Supplementary Table 2; enrichment LFCs come from Supplementary Table 3. Gene membership (~389 genes) follows Supplementary Table 1 (same set as Table 2) — view all included genes. This is published screen data, not output from your AlphaMissense run.

Colored AMBER guide bars mark residues where AMBER found a qualifying guide under your analysis settings (PAM and editing window; default window positions 4–8). Screen LFC can still appear at residues AMBER does not mark: the publication often annotates a broader CBE activity window than AMBER’s default, and one guide’s LFC can be assigned to multiple amino acids via multi-site annotations. Domains and colored AMBER guides are shown again at the bottom of the enrichment figure.
