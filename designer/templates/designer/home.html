Design of Base-Editing guide RNAs

AMBER designs base-editing guide RNAs for a chosen protein and ranks codon outcomes with AlphaMissense. Results also report ESM1b LLR and CADD PHRED scores (highlighted when they pass author cutoffs).

Supports ABE / CBE and PAMs NGG, NG, and NAG. Multi-site guides can be filtered or grouped; guides that span exon–intron boundaries are excluded.

The Guide RNAs that span exon-intron boundaries are automatically excluded, as these would not function correctly in the processed mRNA.
